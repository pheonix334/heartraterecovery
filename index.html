Heart Rate Recovery Calculator

A key marker of cardiovascular fitness

Heart rate recovery (HRR) measures how quickly your heart rate drops in the first minute after you stop exercising. A faster drop generally reflects better cardiovascular fitness and autonomic nervous system function — it's a genuinely useful, well-studied fitness marker, distinct from your target training zones.

Your measurements

Check your pulse (or use a heart rate monitor) immediately after stopping vigorous exercise, then again exactly one minute later during a light rest or gentle cool-down.

How it works

  1. Exercise vigorously for several minutes to raise your heart rate.
  2. Check your heart rate immediately when you stop (your peak).
  3. Rest for exactly one minute, then check it again.
  4. We calculate the difference — the bigger the drop, the more efficiently your cardiovascular system recovers.

Consistency matters more than any single reading. Track this over weeks as your fitness changes, using the same general exercise intensity each time.
